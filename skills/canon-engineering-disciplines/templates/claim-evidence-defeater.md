# Claim, Evidence, and Defeater Matrix

| Claim | Subject identity | Evidence and altitude | Producer and independence | Freshness or expiry | Assumptions | Defeaters or counterevidence | Decision authority | Non-claims |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Verdict vocabulary

- `UNASSESSED`: no adequate evidence has been evaluated.
- `SUPPORTED_UNDER_ASSUMPTIONS`: evidence supports the claim only while listed assumptions hold.
- `BLOCKED`: a live defeater or missing required evidence prevents the decision.
- `SUPPORTED`: required evidence is current, applicable, and no unresolved defeater remains.

Do not map these labels automatically onto a project's merge, release, review, or cutover verdicts.
