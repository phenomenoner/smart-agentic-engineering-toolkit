Retired in 0.5.0. The CLI compatibility bridge duplicated host-native routing, reconstructed policy
and authority through a child process, and could be selected precisely when the native capability
boundary said the requested route was unavailable. Work directly or use an eligible route actually
exposed by the host after the normal delegation gate. Do not shell out to `codex exec` to manufacture
a model or effort lane.
